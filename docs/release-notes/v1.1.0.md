losslessaudiocut 1.1: cut **videos** without re-encoding them. Phone
videos, screen recordings and browser recordings keep every frame and
every audio sample bit for bit; only whole groups of pictures are dropped
or kept, so nothing is ever re-compressed.

## New since 1.0

- **Video cutting**: MP4, QuickTime (`.mov`) and 3GPP (`.3gp`) files with
  H.264, HEVC (also Dolby Vision), AV1, VP9, MPEG-4 Visual, ProRes or APV
  video, and WebM/Matroska files with VP8, VP9, AV1, H.264 or HEVC video,
  are cut with their picture. The sound stays the editor's timeline;
  picture and sound stay in sync to within half an audio packet at every
  join.
  - MP4/MOV/3GP: the start and end of an export are frame-exact (edit
    list); a part after a deleted section starts at a keyframe.
  - WebM/Matroska have no edit list: an export starts at the keyframe
    before the chosen start, sound included.
- **Video preview**: a *Video* pane shows the picture at the cursor or the
  playhead; `,` and `.` step one picture back or forward (Play menu). A
  strip above the time ruler shows the pictures at the keyframes, where a
  cut in the video can be.
- **Sound in videos**: Opus in MP4 (Android), AMR-NB/AMR-WB in 3GP (voice
  notes, older phones), uncompressed PCM in MOV and MP4 (cameras, screen
  recorders).
- `lac info` describes the video track; the open dialog lists `.mov`,
  `.3gp` and `.3g2` files.

## Known limitations

- Players that ignore MP4 edit lists show up to one group of pictures
  more at the start of an exported video.
- HDR pictures are shown without tone mapping in the preview.
- AMR-WB's top band (6.4 to 7 kHz) is noise generated from the stream's
  start, so after a cut it decodes to different noise of the same level,
  as AAC's noise bands do; the export report says so. Within 400 ms after
  a join, AMR and Opus in MP4 decode slightly differently (below one
  16-bit step afterwards), as Opus in Matroska and Speex already did.
- Subtitle tracks and further audio or video tracks are not kept.
